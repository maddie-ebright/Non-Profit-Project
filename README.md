# Sierra Forever | Nonprofit Management Assessment & Strategic Plan

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive organizational assessment and strategic plan for **Sierra Forever**, a nonprofit organization dedicated to educating and inspiring people about the Sierra Nevada and Great Basin public lands.

Completed as part of my MBA coursework at Biola University, this project evaluates Sierra Forever's organizational effectiveness, operational challenges, stakeholder relationships, and long-term sustainability.

Through primary research, stakeholder interviews, and secondary research, I identified organizational strengths, areas for improvement, and actionable strategies to strengthen nonprofit operations and mission impact.

## Research Methodology

The assessment combined qualitative research, organizational analysis, and strategic planning.

**Primary Research**
- Conducted 8 interviews with organizational stakeholders.
- Interviewed executive leadership, a board member, employees, and volunteers.
- Evaluated organizational operations from multiple perspectives.
- Identified recurring challenges and opportunities across stakeholder groups.

**Secondary Research**
- Reviewed organizational resources, reports, and publicly available information.
- Examined nonprofit management practices and strategic planning frameworks.
- Analyzed organizational programs, stakeholder engagement, and fundraising approaches.

## Areas of Analysis

The assessment examined several key components of nonprofit management:

1. **Mission & Vision:** Evaluated organizational purpose, strategic alignment, and mission communication.
2. **Performance Measurement:** Assessed how Sierra Forever measures organizational outputs and long-term outcomes.
3. **Leadership & Governance:** Examined decision-making structures, board involvement, and organizational communication.
4. **Volunteer Management:** Evaluated recruitment, training, retention, and volunteer engagement.
5. **Stakeholder Relationships:** Assessed communication and involvement among employees, volunteers, donors, and community partners.
6. **Competitive Positioning:** Examined target audiences and relationships with other Eastern Sierra nonprofits.
7. **Marketing & Promotion:** Evaluated digital outreach, visitor-center communication, and nonprofit awareness.
8. **Fundraising & Sustainability:** Assessed revenue diversification, donor engagement, and financial resilience.

## Key Findings

The research identified several opportunities for organizational improvement:

- **Performance Measurement:** Sierra Forever collects substantial participation and operational data but has opportunities to better measure long-term educational and stewardship outcomes.
- **Internal Communication:** Valuable information gathered by frontline employees does not always reach organizational leadership.
- **Volunteer Engagement:** Volunteers expressed strong connections to the organization's mission but identified opportunities for improved onboarding, communication, and utilization of professional skills.
- **Organizational Awareness:** Some visitors do not recognize Sierra Forever as a nonprofit or understand how bookstore purchases support its mission.
- **Financial Sustainability:** Diversifying funding sources and strengthening donor relationships could improve long-term organizational resilience.

## Strategic Recommendations

Based on the assessment, I developed recommendations focused on five areas:

**1. Strengthen Performance Measurement**
- Establish consistent organizational performance indicators.
- Improve tracking of educational outcomes.
- Develop centralized reporting processes.

**2. Improve Volunteer Management**
- Standardize volunteer onboarding and training.
- Create systems for identifying volunteer skills and interests.
- Strengthen communication and engagement between volunteer activities.

**3. Enhance Marketing & Communication**
- Increase awareness of Sierra Forever's nonprofit mission.
- Improve coordination between visitor-center operations and digital communication.
- Develop more consistent methods for collecting and sharing organizational success stories.

**4. Support Financial Sustainability**
- Strengthen donor communication and recurring giving opportunities.
- Develop structured fundraising goals.
- Improve communication of organizational accomplishments and financial needs.

**5. Increase Stakeholder Engagement**
- Establish consistent feedback processes.
- Improve communication between frontline employees and leadership.
- Encourage greater stakeholder participation in organizational improvement initiatives.
